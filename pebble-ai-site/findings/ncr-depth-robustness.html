Full coverage of the cycle, a graded depth cost, and what freezing actually buys

Sam Larson

pebble, San Francisco

August 21, 2026  ·  sam@pebbleml.com

abstract

Four eval-only results on the same 98M-parameter, 12-layer DeltaNet LM carrying a Native Composition Reads fast-weight head (K=24 entities, d=25 state), all in the P1b exact-write regime — the relation operator is teacher-forced from the true binding, not learned. (1) Complete coverage: the 15 cycle residues never previously measured read retrieval24_acc 0.9922–1.0 on both frozen arms (primary min 0.9961 / mean 0.9990; compA min 0.9922 / mean 0.9982), which — with the 3 training residues and the 5 already measured — puts all 23 nontrivial positions of the K=24 cycle at 0.99 or better. (2) A real depth cost: at h ∈ {13, 61, 253, 1021, 4093}, every one ≡ 13 (mod 24) and therefore carrying the identical correct answer by construction, accuracy declines monotonically with the number of squarings — a purely numerical property of the O(log h) read, mean −0.0360 (frozen, cosine aux) to −0.3774 (trainable, cosine aux) over 5→11 squarings across 58 checkpoints. (3) An instrument catch: the eval harness hardcoded entity-pool seed 0 while reseeded checkpoints trained with their own pool seeds; this manufactured an apparent complete freeze-vs-trainable separation (p=5.96×10⁻¹¹) that was caught internally and never published. Under the corrected matched-pool instrument all four training recipes compose at 0.978–1.000 (quadrant medians) on their own entity distribution — deep exact composition does not require freezing, which makes the capability claim stronger, not weaker. (4) The residual, pre-registered before it ran: a real but graded freeze ordering emerges off-ceiling — median gap +0.0098 at h=61 (p=1.6×10⁻⁴) rising to +0.1133 at h=4093 (p=4.3×10⁻⁹) — with 4.2% of frozen–trainable pairs still violating the ordering at the deepest point. It is an ordering, never a separation. The interaction is the open mechanism question: a contrastive auxiliary rescues most of a trainable adapter's depth robustness (drift −0.165) where a cosine-only auxiliary does not (−0.377), while frozen arms hold 0.9336–0.9648 at 11 squarings. The wall is unchanged: the model's own SGD-learned write reads at chance in all 58 trainings (165/165 matched-pool readings inside chance ± 3sd). Total spend ≈0.25 GPU-h, eval-only, no new training.

01The construction, and why "depth" here means two different things

The system under test is finding no. 17's: a 98M-parameter, 12-layer DeltaNet language model with a Native Composition Reads head (finding no. 14's mechanism), which writes a relation operator Z in context and reads its h-fold composition at query time by binary exponentiation — O(log h) squarings rather than h applications. Every number on this page is measured in the P1b regime: the operator is teacher-forced from the true binding and substituted into the trained checkpoint's own read path. That is a read-path capability measurement, not a demonstration that the model learns to write the operator — it does not (§05).

The task's ground truth is a single Hamiltonian cycle over K=24 entities. That fact governs what "deeper" can mean, and an archive gate caught us framing it wrongly before any compute was spent: because the cycle closes, the correct answer at hop h depends only on h mod 24, so there are at most 24 distinct outcomes and a proposed "scaling curve to h≈1000" would have been measuring the same handful of answers repeatedly (one proposed rung, h=1021, silently collided with h=61). Two honest experiments survive that constraint, and both were pre-registered before running:

  • Coverage — walk the residues that had never been measured, and ask whether the capability holds at every reachable cycle position or only the ones we happened to test.
  • Physical depth at fixed residue — hold the residue constant at 13 and vary only the number of squarings the read machinery performs. Every depth has the same correct answer by construction, so any spread is numerics, not composition.

02Complete coverage: all 23 nontrivial cycle positions

Three residues (1, 2, 3) appear in training and five more (5, 12, 13, 16, 20) had been measured on earlier ladders. The remaining 15 — h ∈ {4, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 14, 15, 17, 18, 19, 21, 22, 23} — were run as literal depths on both frozen-adapter arms, n=256, eval seed 90210, at ckpt_step 20,000. The pre-registered band for COMPLETE-COVERAGE was ≥0.95 at all 15 residues on both frozen arms; the floor across 30 readings came in at 0.9922.

This is coverage, explicitly not depth scaling: the deepest of these residues is h=23. Meanwhile the model's own learned write (P0) reads 0.019–0.059 against a chance baseline of 0.0417 at every one of those residues — the wall from finding no. 17 holds at every cycle position too.

03Physical depth at fixed residue: a real, graded floating-point cost

The second experiment holds the answer fixed and varies only the machinery. All five hops — 13, 61, 253, 1021, 4093 — are ≡ 13 (mod 24), so on this single-cycle construction they have the identical correct answer; what changes is that the binary-exponentiation read performs 3, 5, 7, 9, and 11 squarings respectively. Any decline is therefore a numerical-precision effect in the read, isolated from composition difficulty. The pre-registered bands were ROBUST (every deeper reading within 0.02 of h=13) or DRIFT (monotone decline >0.02). The verdict is DRIFT: on the two frozen arms, 1.0000 → 0.9219 (primary) and 1.0000 → 0.9062 (compA), strictly monotone over 3→11 squarings.

A follow-up extended that ladder from 3 cells to all 58 trained checkpoints under matched pools, at h ∈ {61, 253, 1021, 4093} — 5 through 11 squarings, still one fixed residue. The four training recipes form a 2×2 (entity adapter frozen or trainable × contrastive+cosine or cosine-only auxiliary loss), and their median curves are the figure below.

Read across the whole set, the drift is real everywhere and its magnitude is recipe-dependent: mean h=61 → h=4093 change of −0.0360 (frozen, cosine-only), −0.0572 (frozen, contrastive+cosine), −0.1654 (trainable, contrastive+cosine), −0.3774 (trainable, cosine-only). 42 of the 55 cells in the 2×2 are strictly monotone non-increasing; the non-monotone cases concentrate in the frozen arms, where the total drops (0.035–0.059) sit close to the 0.0039-per-item resolution of an n=256 eval. The trainable cosine-only arm is cleanly monotone in 8 of 8 cells. The honest sentence is "stable to 0.93–0.96 at physical depth 4093 when the adapter is frozen," not "unaffected by depth."

04The instrument catch, and the reframe it forced

A sensitivity pass during the coverage run found a defect in our own evaluation harness. The eval path constructed its entity pools with a hardcoded seed 0, while the reseeded checkpoints had each been trained with their own seed — and that seed drives the entity train/held-out split. Frozen-adapter arms are immune by construction (the adapter never moved from its initialization, so pool identity is irrelevant), and a matched re-read confirmed it: both verdicts above are unchanged. But arms with a trained entity adapter were being scored off-distribution. Under matched pools the same compB checkpoint reads 0.9883–1.0 across the 15 residues (mean 0.9956) instead of 0.7617–0.9258, and 0.9922 → 0.8945 on the depth ladder instead of 0.8516 → 0.0625.

Every affected number was quarantined and the full battery re-scored: 58 cells, matched pools, 0.132 GPU-h. The instrument was trusted only after its seed-0 column reproduced the numbers of record 52/52 bit-for-bit. The result of the re-score is a retraction and a reframe:

Under the defective instrument the frozen and trainable arms separated completely — every frozen cell above every trainable cell, U = 754/754, p = 1.34×10⁻¹⁰ (the effect recorded internally at p = 5.96×10⁻¹¹). Under matched pools that collapses: U = 596, 216 of 754 pairs violating, median gap +0.0098, a 32× shrink, at ceiling. The apparent freeze effect was an entity-pool generalization artifact that compounds with composition depth — which is exactly why it impersonated a depth-dependent freeze effect. The retracted separation was internal only; finding no. 17, the one published result touching these checkpoints, uses the trivially-matched seed-0 cell and makes only claims that survive intact.

what the catch bought Our own verification layer caught our own artifact before it reached publication, and the capability claim came out stronger: under a sound instrument, all four training recipes compose deeply under exact write — 0.978–1.000 (quadrant medians) at h=61 on their own entity distribution. Deep exact composition does not require freezing the entity adapter. The positive statement of what freezing actually does: frozen adapters are entity-pool-agnostic, while trained adapters specialize to their training entities, and that specialization cost compounds with composition depth when evaluated off-pool.

05The residual ordering is real, graded, and depth-amplified

The re-score left a small ordering behind — frozen ≥ trainable-contrastive ≥ trainable-cosine — but at h=61 it is compressed against the ceiling, so it was pre-registered as a question with explicit bands before the deep ladder ran: RESIDUAL-CONFIRMED required a frozen-vs-trainable median gap >0.05 and Mann–Whitney p <0.01 at some h ≥ 253. Both conditions are met at h=1021 and h=4093.

The last column is the one that governs how this may be phrased. Even at the deepest point, 4.2% of frozen–trainable checkpoint pairs run the wrong way; the distributions overlap. This is a graded ordering, not a separation — categorically different from the 0-out-of-754 claim retracted in §04, and it must never be written as separation.

What makes the ordering interesting is that it is not a clean main effect. At h=4093 the freeze main effect is +0.1133, and the auxiliary-loss main effect is 0.0137 in magnitude — essentially nothing. But split within conditions: switching the trainable arm from contrastive+cosine to cosine-only costs 0.2832 (0.8906 → 0.6074), while the same switch within the frozen arms is +0.0312 in the other direction (0.9336 → 0.9648). In other words the contrastive auxiliary rescues most of a trainable adapter's depth robustness, and freezing makes the choice of auxiliary nearly irrelevant (compB vs compD p = 7.5×10⁻⁴; every frozen-vs-trainable pairwise comparison p ≤ 6.3×10⁻⁴). Why the contrastive term substitutes for freezing is an open mechanism question, pre-registered as a secondary axis of the next sweep rather than answered here.

The wall did not move. Under matched pools, the model's own SGD-learned write reads at chance in every one of the 58 trainings — 165/165 readings between 0.0117 and 0.0742 against chance 0.0417, all inside chance ± 3sd (binomial sd 0.0125 at n=256), plus 58/58 at h=61 on the deep ladder. Everything on this page is a property of the read path given a correct operator; producing that operator by learning remains the open problem finding no. 17 surfaced.

06Limitations — read before citing

  • P1b is exact teacher-forced operator substitution, not a learned write. Every accuracy on this page is measured with the relation operator computed from the true binding and fed into the trained checkpoint's read path. This is a read-path capability result. The same checkpoints' own writes read at chance (§05).
  • A synthetic entity-binding task grafted into a real LM. The construction exists to exercise the NCR head; nothing here claims the backbone uses this circuit in ordinary language modeling.
  • One K=24 single-cycle construction. Because the ground truth is a single Hamiltonian 24-cycle, coverage means 23 nontrivial positions and nothing beyond, and depths above 24 are re-readings of a residue rather than new compositional information. Whether any of this holds at other K is untested — it is the next wave, not a result.
  • The depth curves isolate numerics, and only numerics. Every hop in the ladder shares one answer by construction. The decline is a floating-point property of repeated squaring in this implementation; it says nothing about whether deeper compositions would be harder.
  • Graded, overlapping distributions. The freeze ordering is significant and depth-amplified but not separating (4.2% violating pairs at h=4093). Cite it as an ordering with an effect size, never as a categorical difference.
  • Resolution floor. At n=256 one item is 0.0039, and several frozen-arm non-monotonicities in §03 sit within a few items of that floor. The original DRIFT band trichotomy carried no noise allowance and should not be reused without a pre-registered tolerance.
  • Single base architecture and one eval seed. 98M-parameter, 12-layer DeltaNet throughout; n=256 and eval seed 90210 on every reading; seed variation is across the 58 training checkpoints, not across eval draws.
  • Three of the 58 cells predate the config flags that define the 2×2 (the mob_g3b24_* checkpoints). They are excluded from every quadrant statistic and reported separately in the archive; they behave like the trainable cosine-only arm.
  • Eval-only. No model was trained for this note. Realized spend: 0.019 + 0.132 + 0.098 ≈ 0.25 GPU-h across the three waves.
