The breadth curve: exact composition holds from K=12 to K=40, and the learning gap widens

Sam Larson

pebble, San Francisco

August 22, 2026  ·  sam@pebbleml.com

abstract

A pre-registered breadth-scaling sweep on the 98M-parameter, 12-layer DeltaNet LM of findings no. 17–18, carrying a Native Composition Reads fast-weight head: eight values of the pair (K, d = K+1) from (12,13) to (40,41), two training recipes, three seeds each — 48 cells of record, matched entity pools, n=256, eval seed 90210, ckpt_step 20,000. (1) CAPABILITY-HOLDS at every K. In the P1b exact-write regime — exact teacher-forced operator substitution, the relation operator computed from the true binding and fed into the trained checkpoint's own read path — chance-corrected accuracy κ at the antipodal top rung is ≥0.9708 in all 48 cells, and at the fixed-effective-distance control ≥0.97 in 47 of 48 (floor 0.9470). The number of squarings the read performs is held identical across K, so the graded floating-point depth cost measured in no. 18 cannot confound the breadth axis. The curve ends at K=40 by construction: K=44's antipodal probe cannot be built inside the matched squaring band, which needs 3K/2 ≤ 63. That is a design limit, not a measured capability limit. (2) The wall is breached at K=12 only, and the structure is the finding. The model's own SGD-learned write (P0) reads 0.1211–0.1484 against chance 0.0833 at h=1 at K=12 (κ 0.041–0.071) — 5 of 6 cells above chance + 3 binomial sd, and reproduced at an independent eval draw (seed 31337, both re-measured cells 0.1406). Every deep hop reads chance at every K, and the h=1 exceedance rate falls 5/6 → 1/6 → 1/6 and is 0/6 from K=24 on, out to K=40. Four single-cell excursions at K=16, 20, 24 and 40 all failed to reproduce on re-measure and are recorded as outliers under the pre-registered clause. (3) The freeze ordering is depth-dependent, and breadth-stable. At the primary 5-squaring readout it is negligible — stratified within-K exact permutation T = 32/54 over the original six strata (43/72 over all eight, against a locked threshold of 53), median within-K gap +0.0020. A pre-registered eval-only depth extension then re-scored all 48 cells at 5, 7, 9 and 11 squarings on identical-ground-truth ladders: at 11 squarings the ordering is ORDERING-ROBUST-CONFIRMED — T = 61.5/72 against that same threshold, two-sided exact p = 3.071×10⁻⁵, leave-one-stratum-out clearing everywhere, and both frontier strata perfectly ordered (18/18 pairs, stated as measured). Combined: the capability-vs-learning separation widens with breadth, and freezing buys depth-robustness that is breadth-stable. 42 training cells + 6 eval-only anchor re-scores, 0 failures, ≈39.1 GPU-h measured; independent audits and a pre-launch resource red-team caught two launch-losing FATALs, 6 MAJOR findings and one stale pre-registered hypothesis before a single cell ran.

01What the curve varies — and the two things it deliberately holds fixed

The system under test is finding no. 17's: a 98M-parameter, 12-layer DeltaNet language model with a Native Composition Reads head (no. 14's mechanism) that writes a relation operator Z in context and reads its h-fold composition at query time by binary exponentiation — O(log h) squarings rather than h applications. Two regimes are measured on every cell. P1b is exact teacher-forced operator substitution: the operator is computed in closed form from the true binding and substituted into the trained checkpoint's own read path — a read-path capability measurement. P0 is the model's own SGD-learned write, read through the identical path. Findings 17 and 18 established both at a single breadth: P1b composes exactly, P0 never leaves chance.

This note asks whether that picture is a property of K=24 or a property of the mechanism. The axis is binding breadth: how many entity→entity bindings the single written operator must carry. Eight points, K ∈ {12, 16, 20, 24, 28, 32, 36, 40}, a 3.3× range. The last two, K=36 and K=40, were added as a frontier extension after the first six came back flat, with the null outcome — no frontier — pre-registered before they ran.

The variable is the pair (K, d = K+1), never K alone. This program has a hard precedent for that: in the toy harness, K=24 was dead at state dimension d=48 and healthy at d=25 — the operator dimension, not the binding count, was doing the work (no. 16). Every point here therefore fixes d at the tight-spare mapping K+1 and moves both together, and every axis label says so. Nothing in this note separates breadth from state dimension; that is a scope limit, stated up front and repeated in §08.

Two things are held fixed on purpose, and both were fixed in the design before any cell ran:

  • The number of squarings. Finding no. 18 measured a real, monotone floating-point drift in the O(log h) read itself — accuracy declining 1.0000 → 0.9219 over 3→11 squarings at identical ground truth. If the ladder at each K used a different number of squarings, a decline along the K axis could be that numerical cost rather than breadth. Every ladder here runs the same six-rung profile (2, 3, 4, 4, 5, 5), and every top rung sits at exactly 5 squarings.
  • The hardness of the query. The ground truth is a single Hamiltonian K-cycle, so the answer at hop h depends only on h mod K. The top rung at each K is chosen antipodal — residue exactly K/2, the maximum reachable distance on the cycle, the hardest query available at that breadth. Every rung's residue is distinct, nonzero, and not one of the three training residues; a table-level assertion fails at import if the derived ladder and the rule disagree.

The antipodal choice creates its own confound — effective distance at the top rung is K/2, so it grows with K — and the design carries the control for it: h_fix, a separate labelled probe at effective distance 4 at every K, at the same 5 squarings. If capability declined with K at the top rung but not at h_fix, the decline would be depth, not breadth.

Those two constraints are also what ends the curve at K=40, and the reason is arithmetic, not empirical. The ladder rule takes the top rung to be the smallest h in [32, 63] with h ≡ K/2 (mod K) — the [32, 63] window is exactly the band that costs 5 squarings, the count held fixed across the whole curve. Such an h exists only when 3K/2 ≤ 63, i.e. K ≤ 42. At K=44 the derivation raises rather than returning a ladder: the antipodal probe is construction-impossible inside the matched band, and the only ways past it — a different squaring count, or a non-antipodal top rung — would break one of the two things the design holds fixed and make the K axis uninterpretable. So K=44 was dropped at design time, and every figure and table here stops at K=40 for that reason. It is a design limit of this instrument, not a measured capability limit. Nothing on this page licenses a claim about what happens at K=44 and above; extending the curve means building a different (and separately pre-registered) ladder.

All accuracies are reported as κ = (acc − 1/K)/(1 − 1/K), chance-corrected. That is not cosmetic: the raw-accuracy bar implied by a fixed margin-over-chance threshold is monotone in K, and the pre-launch audit measured that a flat 0.980-accuracy model would have failed such a bar only at K=12 — manufacturing a "frontier at K=12" out of a flat model (§07).

02Curve 1: exact composition is flat across breadth

The pre-registered band was CAPABILITY-HOLDS(K) = κ ≥ 0.90 at the top rung on ≥2 of 3 frozen seeds, and CAPABILITY-HOLDS (curve) = that at every K. The alternative, FRONTIER-AT-K* — the smallest K where it fails — was pre-registered as the more interesting outcome and would have been reported as a positive result about a measurable breadth limit. It did not occur, at the original six points and then again at the two frontier points added to hunt for it. All 48 cells clear the bar at both probes, on both recipes, with a wide margin.

The two frontier points are worth stating as their own result, because they were run to break the curve. The extension pre-registered its own null expectations — capability holds at both new K, wall holds at both — precisely so that a confirmation could not be narrated as a discovery afterwards. Both confirmed exactly: 12/12 cells at ceiling, 0 failures, 12/12 clean on the convergence gate. The honest headline of that wave is no frontier found, and the curve then stops for the arithmetic reason in §01 rather than because anything failed.

One prior deserves naming because it predicted otherwise. The toy harness's own K record is a free-write record: FRONTIER-AT-K*=30, and K=32 far-depth death at h ≈ 5–6 (no. 16, where the K-axis was closed at K=32 on trainability grounds). The design treated that prior as a positive control on the wall curve rather than a threat to the capability curve, and ran K=32 first and alone as the calibration gate for exactly that reason. The gate cleared at ceiling — CE 11.037 → 4.528, in-distribution κ = 1.000 at h ∈ {1,2,3}, deep κ = 0.9960 at h=48 — and the K=32 risk did not materialize in the exact-write regime.

03Curve 2: the wall, and the one place it cracks

The wall is the other half of the separation: the same checkpoints' own SGD-learned writes, read through the same path. The pre-registered band is per-K — every P0 reading inside chance ± 3 binomial sd (0.0173 at K=12, n=256, down to 0.0109 at K=32) — with WALL-BREACHED-AT-K requiring a reading above band replicated across ≥2 seeds, and an explicit clause that a single-seed excursion is an outlier to re-measure, not a breach.

The verdict is WALL-BREACHED-AT-K=12, reported exactly as pre-registered — and the shape of the breach is the interesting part. It is confined to h=1: the shallowest hop, and one of the three hops the model is actually trained on. Five of the six K=12 cells sit above chance + 3 sd there (0.1387 median vs 0.0833 chance is ≈3.2 sd, about 14 extra correct answers out of 256), both recipes are represented among them, and an independent eval draw at a fresh seed reproduces it on both cells re-measured. At h=2 and h=3 — also training hops — every cell at every K is inside the band, and across the whole curve 334 of 336 deep-hop readings are in band; both exceptions re-measured away (below).

So the honest sentence is: at the smallest breadth tested, SGD's own writes capture a weak one-hop toehold — enough to beat chance at the shallowest training hop, nothing at all beyond it. By K=24 even that is gone, and it stays gone all the way out: the h=1 exceedance count runs 5/6, 1/6, 1/6, 0/6, 0/6, 0/6, 0/6, 0/6, and the median chance-corrected value decays 0.0604 → 0.0354 → 0.0111 → 0.0075 → 0.0014 → −0.0060 → +0.0016 → −0.0036. Read the tail of that sequence as noise around zero, not as structure — from K=28 on the medians are a few thousandths either side of chance and their ordering carries no information. The K=12 h=1 toehold remains the only crack anywhere in K=12→40. It is a toehold, not partial competence, and it does not compose.

Four single-cell excursions were handled by the clause written before the run, not after it: K=16 seed 1 at h=1 (0.1250), K=20 seed 1 at h=1 (0.1094), the K=24 anchor's h=52 (0.0859), and — in the frontier wave — one K=40 trainable cell's h=4 reading of 0.0586 against a band top of 0.0543. Each was re-measured on an independent eval draw and each came back inside the band (0.0977 / 0.0664 / 0.0430; the K=40 cell's maximum across all ten hops on re-measure is 0.0391, with h=4 itself at 0.0156). All four are recorded as outliers, and WALL-HOLDS(K) stands at K = 16, 20, 24, 28, 32, 36 and 40.

04The separation, and why it widens

Put the two curves on one axis and the result is the point of the whole sweep. Above, the exact-write read holds ceiling across a 3.3× range of breadth. Below, the model's own write is at chance everywhere except one hop at one K — and the exception shrinks as breadth grows.

what the curve says Across a 3.3× range of binding breadth, the gap between what the read path can do with a correct operator and what gradient descent puts into that operator does not close — it opens. At K=12, exact write reads κ ≈ 1.0 and the learned write reads κ ≈ 0.06 at one shallow hop and 0 everywhere else. At K=40, exact write still reads κ ≈ 0.992 and the learned write reads κ ≈ −0.004 at that same hop. The capability is breadth-insensitive in this range; the learning failure is not — it gets more total. That is a statement about the write problem, not about the architecture's ceiling — and the curve stops at K=40 because the probe cannot be built past it, not because anything bent.

05Curve 3: the freeze ordering is absent at this depth — as predicted

Finding no. 18 reported a real but graded frozen-vs-trainable ordering that only becomes measurable off-ceiling, at 9–11 squarings (median gap +0.0098 at 5 squarings rising to +0.1133 at 11). This sweep's primary readout sits at exactly 5 squarings, so the pre-registered expectation was that the ordering would be compressed against the ceiling here. It is.

06The adjudication at depth: the ordering is confirmed, and it is breadth-stable

The depth extension is eval-only and cheap — no new training, all 48 existing checkpoints re-scored in 0.085 GPU-h total. Its instrument is built to remove the one thing that could fake a depth effect. At each K it derives four rungs that all share the same residue r_fix = 4 (mod K) — so all four ask the identical question of the identical ground truth, by construction — while costing 5, 7, 9 and 11 squarings respectively. At K=40, for instance, the rungs are h = 44, 164, 524, 2084; at K=12 they are 40, 136, 520, 2056. Same answer, four different amounts of repeated squaring, at all eight breadths. Any decline along that ladder is the arithmetic of the O(log h) read, not the difficulty of the query.

Two questions were registered before the pass, with bands: does the ordering that no. 18 found at K=24 open uniformly across breadth, and is the depth cost itself K-dependent?

The threshold was fixed before these numbers existed: T ≥ 53 of 72, re-enumerated exactly on the eight-stratum permutation null (two-sided exact p at the threshold itself = 0.009868) and written into the design at launch time. That matters because 53 is a genuine knife-edge — two null-like strata would land the total at 52.5 and fail; two replicating strata would land it at 58 and pass. The observed T = 61.5 clears it, at two-sided exact p = 3.071×10⁻⁵. Verdict of record: ORDERING-ROBUST-CONFIRMED.

Two robustness legs were registered with it, and both hold. Leave-one-stratum-out: dropping any single K and re-running the test on the remaining seven leaves T between 52.5 and 55.5 of 63 — 60.0 to 63.4 rescaled to the eight-stratum scale — so the result clears the threshold whichever breadth you delete. No single stratum is carrying it. Continuity with the earlier wave: restricted to the original six strata the same statistic reads 43.5 of 54, exactly the number the earlier six-stratum pass reported, so the two waves are the same measurement and not a re-analysis.

And the ordering strengthens with breadth: the within-K gap climbs from +0.0000 at K=12 to +0.0683 and +0.0841 at K=36 and K=40, and in both frontier strata every frozen cell beats every trainable cell — 18 of 18 pairs, a literal separation in those two strata, stated as measured. (K=24 and K=28 read 9/9 too.) But for the effect as a whole this remains a graded ordering, not a separation: four of the eight strata still contain violating pairs — K=12, 16, 20 and 32 — and the pooled statistic is a rank count, not a gap.

The honest part: the statistic peaks at 9 squarings, not 11

Across the four depths the same test reads T = 48.0 (5 sq) → 57.0 (7 sq) → 65.0 (9 sq) → 61.5 (11 sq). It rises with depth, as no. 18 predicted — and then dips at the deepest rung. The dip is traceable: one frozen cell at K=32 reads κ 0.8669 at 11 squarings, below all three trainable cells in its own stratum, which alone costs that stratum three of its nine pairs. It is a weak seed, flagged in the earlier six-stratum pass, not a mechanism. But the consequence is worth stating plainly rather than smoothing: the ordering statistic is not monotone in depth, and a single cell still moves it by several points. That is exactly what "graded ordering" means, and it is why the claim here is a rank-order confirmation and not a separation claim.

The depth cost: flat when frozen, growing when trainable

The second registered question was whether the depth drift itself is K-dependent. The pinned band compares each K's drift against the K=24 reference on the median convention; the largest deviation anywhere is 0.0430, inside the ±0.05 window, so DRIFT-K-INDEPENDENT passes with all eight K, exactly as pinned. The frozen arm's depth cost is genuinely flat across the whole 3.3× range — it sits between −0.0195 and −0.0469 with no trend in K. The trainable arm's is not: it grows roughly monotonically with breadth, from −0.0195 at K=12 to −0.0938 at K=40. The pinned band did not catch this because it measures distance from the K=24 reference, not slope, and a slow monotone drift stays inside a ±0.05 window for a long time. So the precise statement of record is: "K-independent" is true as pinned, and true of the frozen arm outright; for the trainable arm it is a statement about this K range and nothing more. Whether the trainable arm's depth cost keeps growing past K=40 is a pre-registerable future question, not a claim of this record, and no number here should be read as an extrapolation of it.

Put together with the ordering result, the paper sentence gains a dimension: freezing buys depth-robustness that is breadth-stable, and trainable adapters pay a depth cost that grows with breadth.

The wall at depth

The same pass re-read the learned write at the deepest rung, 11 squarings, in every cell. 48 of 48 are inside the pre-registered chance band. The closest approach anywhere is K=36, whose maximum reading is 0.0547 against a band top of 0.0586 — worth a glance on any future re-measure, and still in band. Combined with §03: across breadth and across depth, the only reading that has ever left the band and stayed out is the K=12 h=1 toehold.

07What the gauntlet caught before a single cell ran

At the 10–50 GPU-h ceremony tier this program requires an independent code audit and a pre-launch resource/placement red-team. The build agent's own end-to-end smoke caught two launch-losing FATALs first: a sequence-length assertion that fails at K=12 and K=16, and a hard-coded h=61 dictionary key — a K=24 literal — that would have raised KeyError at the first evaluation of every cell, burning the entire ledger to nothing. Only the run through the literal spec command line saw the second; module-level smoke was blind to it, so both instruments are now required on every revision.

The audit then returned REV-REQUIRED with 1 FATAL, 6 MAJOR and 6 minor findings, and the red-team returned BLOCK with 2 launch-losing findings. The four that changed what this page says:

  • FATAL — the K=24 anchor could not have run at all. The battery's runner-tag guard rejected the older checkpoints the anchor re-scores. Fixed with a narrow allowlist for read-only scoring (the resume guard stays strict) and verified by execution before launch: the anchor cell reads 0.9961 / κ 0.9959 at its top rung.
  • MAJOR — the statistics would have hidden a real effect. The design's planned pooled 15-vs-15 Mann–Whitney analyzed a blocked design as if it were unblocked. The audit measured the consequence rather than arguing it: a unanimous within-K frozen-beats-trainable ordering (44.5 of 45 wins) still yields a pooled p = 0.361, because κ varies far more across K than between recipes within K — while a pure single-K artifact yields p = 0.016. Anti-powered in exactly the regime the sweep existed to probe. Replaced before launch by the stratified within-K exact permutation test §05 reports.
  • MAJOR — the primary band would have manufactured a frontier. Stated on margin-over-chance, the capability bar is monotone in K: a 0.90 margin demands raw accuracy 0.9833 at K=12 but only 0.9313 at K=32. A flat 0.980-accuracy model would therefore have failed only at K=12, and the sweep would have emitted FRONTIER-AT-K*=12 as a pre-registered output of a completely flat capability. Re-elected onto κ, whose implied raw-accuracy span across the range is 0.0052. Margin is still recorded in every output JSON.
  • MAJOR / launch-losing — the calibration gate was not machine-enforced. The gate was convention-only, and the box's own idle-GPU refill daemon fires on exactly the predicate calibration creates (2 GPUs busy, 6 idle, queue empty): within one cron tick it would have promoted the gated sweep specs into the run queue and launched the full 25.6 GPU-h without the gate ever being read. Fixed with a licence sentinel prefixed into every gated command and by staging those specs outside the queue directory entirely. The red-team's other launch-losing find was a pair of duplicate specs byte-identical to the calibration cells and sharing their checkpoint paths — deleted, which also trimmed the ledger from 32 cells to 30.

The frontier extension went through the same machine a second time, and it caught two things worth naming. The audit re-derived both new ladders without importing the design's config — the rule alone reproduces the two new ladders and all six of record, and derive_ladder(44) raises, which is how the K=44 construction limit in §01 is a verified fact rather than an assertion. It also found that all twelve new job specs still carried a stale pre-registered hypothesis: a claim about the fixed-distance probe that a correction to the earlier wave had already falsified. Shipping it would have meant scoring the frontier against a hypothesis known to be wrong. Fixed before launch, with the diff verified at exactly one line in each of the twelve files. One thing was disclosed rather than fixed: twelve cells on eight GPUs leaves a scheduling tail that idles four of them for ≈4.45 GPU-h, and no audited backfill existed — so none was invented for the sake of occupancy.

The runs themselves were uneventful, which is the point: 42 training cells (0.78–1.16 GPU-h each, 20,000 steps, all COMPLETED), 6 eval-only anchor re-scores, 6 independent-draw re-measures, and the eval-only depth extension over all 48 cells — 0 failures anywhere. Recorded training cost 38.79 GPU-h (25.82 for the first two waves against a 25.63 ledger; 12.97 for the frontier wave against a 13.37 ledger), plus ≈0.27 GPU-h across every eval battery including the depth extension — ≈39.1 GPU-h all in for the whole breadth chapter.

08Limitations — read before citing

  • P1b is exact teacher-forced operator substitution, not a learned write. Every capability number on this page is measured with the relation operator computed in closed form from the true binding and fed into the trained checkpoint's read path. It is a read-path capability result. The same checkpoints' own writes are the wall curve in §03.
  • A synthetic entity-binding task grafted into a real LM. The construction exists to exercise the NCR head. Nothing here claims the backbone uses this circuit in ordinary language modeling.
  • d = K+1 co-varies with K throughout. The variable is the pair, never K alone — this design cannot attribute the flat curve to breadth-invariance as opposed to the tight-spare mapping tracking breadth. A d-sweep at fixed K is a different experiment.
  • Three seeds per (K, recipe). A per-K Mann–Whitney on 3 vs 3 has a minimum attainable two-sided p of 0.10, so no per-K significance claim is made or is even possible here; that is why the ordering test is stratified across K. This was disclosed in the design before the run, not discovered at harvest.
  • One backbone. A single 98M-parameter, 12-layer DeltaNet at every point; the NCR head is the only part that changes with K (170,125 → 177,321 parameters from K=12 to K=40). Both recipes use the same contrastive+cosine auxiliary and differ only in whether the entity adapter is frozen.
  • The K=24 point is eval-only. It re-scores six existing checkpoints, trained earlier under a different harness, on this design's ladder — hence its own stratum in §05's test. It is an anchor to the 58-cell record, not a fresh training point.
  • The K=12 breach is small and in-distribution. ≈3.2 sd, ≈14 extra correct answers out of 256, at one of the three training hops. It reproduces at a fresh eval draw and it is a real departure from chance — and it is a toehold, not partial composition. Read as "SGD gets a sliver of the one-hop lookup at the smallest breadth," never as "the write partially works."
  • Both flat means both flat. The fixed-distance control sits at effective distance 4 at every K. It rules out the reading that the flat top-rung curve is probe easiness; it does not establish breadth-invariance at every intermediate depth.
  • The curve ends at K=40 for a construction reason, and says nothing past it. K=44's antipodal probe cannot be built inside the matched squaring band (it needs 3K/2 ≤ 63). This is a limit of the instrument, not a measured capability limit — but it is still a limit: no reading on this page licenses any claim about K ≥ 44 in either direction.
  • “K-independent depth cost” is scoped for the trainable arm. The pinned band measures each K's drift against the K=24 reference, not its slope. The frozen arm is flat across all eight breadths with no trend; the trainable arm's depth cost grows roughly monotonically (−0.0195 at K=12 to −0.0938 at K=40). The verdict passes as pinned, and for the trainable arm it is a statement about this K range only. Nothing here extrapolates it.
  • The ordering statistic is not monotone in depth. It reads T = 48.0 / 57.0 / 65.0 / 61.5 at 5 / 7 / 9 / 11 squarings — peaking at 9, with one weak frozen seed at K=32 accounting for the dip at 11. The confirmed claim is a rank-order confirmation at the deepest rung, not a monotone depth law, and single cells still move the statistic by several points.
  • One eval seed for the curve of record (90210, n=256); the six re-measures use 31337. Seed variation is across training checkpoints, not across eval draws.
  • Single-cycle ground truth. Answers depend only on h mod K, so a "depth" is a residue. Every rung's residue is distinct, nonzero and non-training by construction, and the derived ladder is asserted against its rule at import — but this is not a task where arbitrarily deep hops carry new compositional information.
