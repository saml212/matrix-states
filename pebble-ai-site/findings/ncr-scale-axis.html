The capability separation survives 4× scale — and its moat widens

Sam Larson

pebble, San Francisco

August 22, 2026  ·  amended August 23, 2026 — the attribution arm and its constant-LR control are in; every gate on this page is discharged  ·  sam@pebbleml.com

abstract

The breadth chapter (no. 19) closed at one model size. This wave carries the same construction to a backbone with 4.008× the parameters — 98M → 392M — porting four of the eight breadths: K ∈ {16, 24, 32, 40}, d = K+1, two recipes, three seeds, 24 training cells (all COMPLETED at step 20,000, 0 failures) and 50 evaluation cells. Every battery is identical per K across the two scales, so the only thing that moves is parameter count. All accuracies are chance-corrected, κ = (acc − 1/K)/(1 − 1/K). (1) The capability does not move. At 392M the frozen arm reads κ 0.9839–1.0000 in all 12 cells at the antipodal top rung — 3/3 seeds over the 0.90 bar at every K. The pre-registered cross-scale test's two forms disagree (T = 17.0/72 at 8 strata, 14.5/54 at 6); per the rule fixed before the data the 6-stratum form governs, so the verdict is NO DETECTABLE DIRECTIONAL SHIFT at 4× parameters, with 6 of 8 per-K cells SCALE-STABLE. (2) The wall holds at both scales. The same checkpoints' own SGD-learned write sits inside its pre-registered chance band in 238 of 240 first-draw readings, and both single-seed excursions re-measured away at an independent eval draw under the clause written before the run — WALL-SCALE-STABLE. (3) The freeze ordering strengthens to a perfect separation. The within-scale statistic reads TW = 36/36 at both readouts at 392M — every frozen seed beats every trainable seed at every K — two-sided exact p = 1.25×10⁻⁵, leave-one-stratum-out clearing 4/4 at the maximum 27/27. The 98M reference reads 30.5 and 25.0 and fails 2 of 4 leave-one-out subsets. (4) The new science is an interaction. At 392M the trainable arm falls below the capability bar from K=32 on (κ 0.9516/0.8750/0.8065 at K=32; 0.7596/0.8438/0.8798 at K=40) while the frozen arm does not move — and the same fall appears at the fixed-effective-distance control, so it is breadth-driven, not the depth cost. At 98M that same trainable recipe is at ceiling at every K. The architectural recipe — closed-form writes plus frozen adapters — is the part that scales. (5) Two things degrade, and the mandatory control has now split them. Very-deep-read robustness (≥13 squarings, ≈8,200 hops) degrades at 392M in both arms (T = 6.5/72, exact p = 9.5×10⁻⁷, leave-one-out 8/8), and the trainable arm's capability degrades at K ≥ 32. Both carried the design's pre-registered, one-directional token-budget disclosure and both were held behind an extended-steps attribution arm, which has since run — 12 cells to 40,000 steps plus a 2-cell schedule control. K=32 trainable recovers (median κ 0.8750 → 0.9113, 2/2 seeds over the bar): TOKEN-BUDGET-LIMITED, and that scale claim is withdrawn — the pre-registered control took away one of our own verdicts. K=40 trainable does not. The first control was compromised — the resume re-opens the cosine schedule, a 5.5× LR warm restart, and it damaged the cells (0.8438 → 0.5513) — so a constant-LR control (V2′) was run at the same budget: 0.7276 / 0.8598 against parents 0.7596 / 0.8438, unrecovered and undamaged. Not the budget, not the schedule: architectural. The depth tail stands in the STRONG form — all 6 cells move further from zero at 2× tokens, while ten of the twelve extended cells demonstrably trained (marginal CE median −0.125, largest −2.686). Two instrument findings fell out: 5.5× warm restarts destabilize trained NCR adapters at the breadth frontier and nothing else measured here, and at this schedule the extra tokens helped once, were flat four times and hurt seven. The ordering results carry no such caveat: both arms share the identical budget. Totals: 38 training cells at 392M and 76 eval passes in this chapter (≈135.7 GPU-h marginal), inside a program ledger of 106 training cells, ≈145 eval passes, 0 unrecovered failures and ≈180 GPU-h.

01What "4× scale" means here, and what is held fixed

The system is finding no. 17's: a DeltaNet language model carrying a Native Composition Reads head that writes a relation operator Z in context and reads its h-fold composition at query time by repeated squaring — O(log h) squarings rather than h applications. Two regimes are measured on every cell, and the distinction is the whole point of the page:

  • P1b — exact teacher-forced operator substitution. The operator is computed in closed form from the true binding and substituted into the trained checkpoint's own read path. This is a read-path capability measurement, never a learned write. Every capability number below is P1b.
  • P0 — the model's own SGD-learned write, read through the identical path. This is the wall.

The scale axis moves the backbone and nothing else. Measured in the run records, not asserted:

Four of the breadth chapter's eight K are ported: K = 16, 24, 32, 40, always paired with d = K+1. The variable is the pair, never K alone — the same scope limit no. 19 carries. The curve stops at K=40 for the same arithmetic reason it stopped there at 98M: the antipodal top rung must be the smallest h ∈ [32, 63] with h ≡ K/2 (mod K), which needs 3K/2 ≤ 63, and derive_ladder(44) raises. K=44 is construction-impossible in the matched squaring band — a design limit of the instrument, not a measured capability limit, and nothing here licenses a claim about K ≥ 44 in either direction.

One thing is deliberately not held fixed, and the design says so in advance. Steps, batch and sequence length are identical across scales, so the token budget per cell is unchanged while parameters quadruple: 20,000 × 32 × t_in = 81.9M tokens at K=16 up to 183.0M at K=40, which drops tokens-per-parameter from 1.87 at 98M to 0.47 at 392M at K=40. The design's pre-registration turns this into an asymmetry rather than a hole: under-training can only manufacture a degradation. It cannot manufacture stability and it cannot manufacture improvement. So the stable and strengthening results below are, if anything, understated by the mismatch — and every degrading result was gated behind an attribution control, which has since run and is reported in full in §06.

The cross-scale question is a separate, pre-registered test: a stratified exact permutation statistic that counts, over 8 strata (4 K × 2 recipes) and 9 cross-scale seed pairs each, how often the 392M cell exceeds its 98M counterpart. It reads T = 17.0/72 — below the 19 bar, nominally a degradation. But the design pre-registered a sensitivity form and a precedence rule before any data existed: report the same statistic again with the K=24 stratum pair dropped, because those 98M cells are an eval-only anchor trained under an older runner, and if the two forms disagree, the 6-stratum verdict governs. They disagree: 6 strata read T = 14.5/54 against a degradation bar of 12 — inside the band. The verdict of record is therefore NO DETECTABLE DIRECTIONAL SHIFT at 4× parameters, and the disagreement is the headline instrument note, exactly as the rule required.

Two further facts belong with it rather than in a footnote. The 8-stratum reading is tie-heavy — 14 of its 72 pairs are exact ties — because the frozen arm is at ceiling at both scales and a 1.0000-vs-1.0000 pair scores ½; the design had already declared, from the 98M numbers alone, that per-K SCALE-IMPROVES was arithmetically unreachable on this curve (the maximum attainable improvement was 0.0000–0.0122 against a 0.05 threshold, 0 of 8). And the 8-stratum reading survives only 4 of 8 leave-one-stratum-out subsets. Per-K, 6 of 8 cells are SCALE-STABLE within ±0.05; the two that are not are both trainable, at K=32 (−0.1169) and K=40 (−0.1442), and both also fail the κ ≥ 0.90 gate. That is §05's subject — and §06's attribution control, now discharged, keeps only one of the two: K=32 recovers over the bar at double the token budget and is withdrawn as a scale claim, K=40 does not.

03The wall, and the re-measure discipline

The other half of the separation is the same checkpoints' own writes. The pre-registered band is per-K — every P0 reading inside chance ± 3 binomial standard deviations at n=256 — and a breach requires a reading above band replicated across ≥2 seeds; a single-seed excursion is explicitly an outlier to re-measure at an independent eval draw, not a breach. That clause was written before the run and it fired twice here.

Combined with no. 19's record at the same four K, that gives the cross-scale verdict: WALL-SCALE-STABLE — the learned write is at chance at both model sizes, at every ported breadth. A separate within-392M reading extends it deeper: at the deepest rung of the depth-extension ladder (15 squarings, h = 32,772–32,804) the learned write is inside the band in 24 of 24 cells. That reading is labelled in every record as not comparable to the archived 98M P0 at 11 squarings, so it is a within-392M statement only — stated as the records state it.

At 98M the same test on the same four strata reads 30.5 at 11 squarings and 25.0 at the 5-squaring top rung, where the ordering was reported as negligible — invisible at that readout at 98M, perfect at 392M.

The bar deserves its own sentence, because the design made it deliberately hard to clear. Four strata give a raw exact p < 0.01 threshold of T ≥ 30 — which the 98M reference itself clears, by half a pair. The design refused to license a verdict at that margin: it partitioned the grid so that ORDERING-CONFIRMED requires TW > 31.5 and leave-one-stratum-out clearing in ≥3 of 4 subsets, and wrote the consequence down in advance — "at 4 strata, ORDERING-CONFIRMED requires the 392M wave to be strictly more robust than its own 98M reference." A reproduction of the reference would have read INDETERMINATE. The observed 36/36 clears both legs at the ceiling, and the sub-case of record is ORDERING-SCALE-STRENGTHENS: descriptive deltas +5.5 at 11 squarings and +11.0 at h_top.

This result is also the one with no token-budget caveat, and that is not a rhetorical point. Both arms are trained on the identical budget, on the identical data, for the identical number of steps; the comparison is within-scale and the confound cancels. Whatever the fixed budget does to a 392M model, it does to both arms at once.

Three things follow, and the third is the one that matters.

It is graded, and it is in every seed. The trainable arm's medians go 0.9792 → 0.9837 → 0.8750 → 0.8438, and the per-seed spread widens with it: 0.9708–0.9917 at K=16, 0.8065–0.9516 at K=32, 0.7596–0.8798 at K=40. This is not one bad seed. At K=40, no trainable seed clears the 0.90 bar; at K=32, one of three does — and at K=32 the attribution control puts both extended seeds back over it at double the token budget (§06), which is why the interaction's surviving frontier is K=40 and not K=32. Every number in this section is at the 20,000-step budget both scales share.

It is breadth, not depth. The design carries a control for exactly this: h_fix, a probe at effective distance 4 at every K, at the same 5 squarings — so if the fall were the floating-point cost of deeper reads it would not appear there. It appears there: the trainable arm's fixed-distance range over K is 0.1040 against the top rung's 0.1399, the same shape at the same K. The frozen arm's ranges are 0.0080 and 0.0121 — flat on both probes. (Disclosed rather than smoothed: the design defined its three labels for this control on "κ" without naming an arm, and the two arms return different labels. It is reported per-arm rather than elected, and the ambiguity is recorded as a design gap.)

And so the finding is not "big models are worse." The frozen recipe — closed-form operator writes plus a frozen entity adapter — is at ceiling at 392M at every breadth, on both probes, with a flat depth-drift profile (largest deviation from the K=24 reference: 0.0197). The trainable recipe is the one that degrades, and its degradation grows with K (largest deviation 0.3019). Scale does not threaten the architecture; it separates the architecture from the alternative to it. The gap between the two recipes at K=40 is 0.144 κ at 392M and essentially zero at 98M — which is what "the moat widens" means, stated as a measurement. The attribution arm makes that measurement stronger, not weaker (§06): at K=32 the gap closes when the trainable arm is given the tokens it was short of, so K=32 was never a moat point; at K=40 the trainable arm stays down through double the tokens and through a schedule control, and against the frozen arm's own 40,000-step cells (κ 0.9840 / 0.9880) the gap widens to 0.192 κ. The frontier the trainable recipe loses at is architectural, and the frozen recipe does not have it.

the through-line At 4× parameters, everything the recipe promises survives — exact-write capability at ceiling at every breadth, the learned-write wall absolute at both scales, and the freeze ordering strengthening to a perfect separation — while the two things that degrade are the trainable adapter (which the recipe already excludes) and very-deep floating-point read tails (which the prior findings already bound). Both degradations went through the mandatory attribution control, and it split them: the trainable arm's fall at K=32 was the token budget and is withdrawn; its fall at K=40 survived double the tokens and a schedule control, and the deep-tail degradation moved further from zero in 6 of 6 cells at 2× tokens (§06).

06The two degradations — and the gate they sat behind, now discharged

Two curves read materially worse at 392M. The design refused to publish either as a scale claim until a mandatory control had run. It has run — 12 cells extended to 40,000 steps, plus a 2-cell follow-up control — and it split the two verdicts apart: one of them was the token budget and is withdrawn, the other survived the budget and a schedule control and is now the stronger claim on the page.

(a) Very-deep-read robustness degrades in both arms

(b) The trainable arm's capability degrades at K ≥ 32 — and only half of that survived

§05's interaction, read as a cross-scale verdict, gave SCALE-DEGRADES at K=32 trainable (−0.1169) and K=40 trainable (−0.1442), both also failing the κ ≥ 0.90 capability gate at 392M. The control separates them: K=32 was the token budget, K=40 is not. The numbers are below.

The gate, stated as the design states it

K=32: the control took a headline number away, and that is what it was for

Both extended K=32 trainable cells clear the bar — 0.9153 and 0.9073, 2 of 2 — so by the rule fixed before the arm ran, the SCALE-DEGRADES verdict at K=32 trainable is WITHDRAWN and reported as token-budget-limited. Read seed by seed it is exactly what a budget story looks like: the seed that sat below the bar rises through it (0.8750 → 0.9073), while the seed already above it settles slightly inside ceiling territory (0.9516 → 0.9153). Said plainly, because the alternative is spin: this page previously carried a scale claim at K=32 that our own pre-registered control has now removed. The control was written to be able to do that, it did it, and the interaction of §05 is narrower than it looked — the trainable arm's real breadth frontier at 392M is K=40, not K=32.

K=40: a two-stage story, because the first control was compromised

Stage one — the control hurt. The resume mechanism re-opens the runner's linear-warmup-plus-cosine schedule, so the learning rate at the resume point jumps from 3.00×10⁻⁵ (the 20,000-step floor) back to ≈1.66×10⁻⁴ — a 5.5× warm restart, disclosed in the cell specs before the arm ran. At K=40 trainable the extension did not merely fail to rescue the cells, it damaged them: 0.8438 → 0.5513 in median, seed-matched −0.3205 and −0.1803, the largest movement anywhere in the arm. The design's licensing argument — under-training can only manufacture a degradation — assumes the extension is non-harmful; a control that damages the cells is uninformative in precisely the direction the verdict needs. So the adjudication was SCALE-DEGRADES stands, unstrengthened, with the compromise recorded rather than the pre-registered word "strengthened" claimed on a broken control.

Stage two — the schedule ruled out. V2′ re-ran the same two cells, from the same 20,000-step parents, for the same 20,000 marginal steps, at a constant 3.00×10⁻⁵ — the restart removed and nothing else changed. It reads 0.7276 / 0.8598 against parents of 0.7596 / 0.8438: unrecovered (0 of 2 over the bar) and undamaged (seed-matched −0.0321 and +0.0160, inside the parent band) — the pre-registered middle branch exactly. Therefore the K=40 trainable degradation is not the token budget (V2′ doubled it) and not the schedule (V2′ removed the restart). It is architectural at 392M, and it is the one place on this page where the trainable arm genuinely loses to scale. The learned-write wall holds in both V2′ cells (maximum P0 0.0391 and 0.0469 against a band top of 0.0543).

The depth tail: more budget trained the models and bought no robustness

The six depth-tail cells recover nowhere, and the marginal cross-entropy says the extra budget was not wasted on them: ten of the twelve extended cells moved training cross-entropy down over the marginal 20,000 steps — median −0.125, largest −2.686 at the most token-starved cells — while κ at 13 squarings moved away from its 98M twin in 6 of 6. More tokens demonstrably train these models. They do not buy back the deep tail of the O(log h) read. The two cells whose CE did not fall (+0.026 and +0.030, both at K=40) tripped a pre-registered "the marginal segment must reduce CE" clause; it was ruled mis-scoped for a resumed segment already at a plateau, and recorded as a scoping decision rather than quietly edited — a flat CE there is itself attribution evidence, since it says the doubled budget moved training loss nowhere in those cells. One disclosure: restricting the 98M twin to the same two seeds flips the verdict at frozen K=32 only — the cell nearest the bar — and the 2-versus-3-seed convention gap is recorded as this chapter's third design gap.

Two instrument findings the arm produced on the way

(i) 5.5× learning-rate warm restarts destabilize trained NCR adapters at the breadth frontier. The V2/V2′ pair is a clean two-cell experiment in its own right — same checkpoints, same marginal budget, same data, same seeds, only the schedule differs — and the contrast is −0.3205 / −0.1803 under the restart against −0.0321 / +0.0160 at constant LR. The damage is also specific: under the identical restart every other extended cell stays within ±0.04 at h_top (K=16 trainable −0.0083 / +0.0208; K=24 −0.0041 / −0.0041; K=32 −0.0363 / +0.0323; K=32 frozen +0.0040 / +0.0040; K=40 frozen −0.0040 / −0.0080). A trained trainable adapter at the widest breadth is the fragile object; frozen adapters shrug it off. The practical consequence, for this model family: a resume that re-opens a cosine schedule is not a neutral way to buy more tokens.

The two results that did need it now have it. One is withdrawn. The other is stronger than when it was written.

07What the gauntlet caught, and what the wave cost

The design went through five rounds — an initial draft, an adversarial attack round, a revision, an independent verification round, and a second revision — surfacing four FATAL-class defects, all discharged before a GPU was touched. Two are worth naming because of what they would have cost:

  • A pricing ratio computed across two incompatible instruments. The pre-launch cost gate divided a fixed-length timing probe by a realized wall-clock figure — two instruments this program's own archive measures as 1.55× apart, because per-arm synchronization kills pipelining. The computed ratio would have landed at 5.39–6.20 against an abort threshold of 5.0, across the entire plausible range: the wave would have aborted itself for a reason that was an artifact of the measurement. The fix was a like-for-like probe-÷-probe ratio, purchased with two 98M timing probes costing ≈0.02 GPU-h. The defect was created by a fix adopted one round earlier — which is the case for having a verification round at all.
  • A recorder that would have logged half the parameter count. The build caught a provenance constant carried over from the 98M configuration that would have written half the true integrator parameter count into every 392M record. The corrected values are visible in the raws and are exactly double the 98M ones at every K (52,224 / 76,800 / 101,376 / 125,952 against 26,112 / 38,400 / 50,688 / 62,976), as the width doubling requires.

The independent build audit then returned PASS-LAUNCH-RELEASED with 0 FATAL, 5 MAJOR, 7 minor and 2 process findings, having re-executed every number in the build — most reproduced bit-identically. Its most useful finding was pre-emptive: an archived timing pin was stale by 46.5%, which it recorded before the pre-launch measurement stage ran, so that the expected cross-check failure would read as a stale baseline rather than a live fault. It also found, for the third time in this codebase, an inverted instrument identification in a design document — a recurring defect class now tracked as such.

The launch was gated on measurement, not on argument. A pre-launch stage measured the real per-step costs on the real hardware: eight-way homogeneous contention came back at R₈ = 1.0026 (0.478638 s/step under 8-way occupancy against 0.477414 solo — contention-free for this wave, resolving an older 5.5× anomaly as a heterogeneous-mix artifact), and the 392M-vs-98M per-step ratio came back at 3.899 at K=24 and 4.4725 at K=40, inside the pre-registered (4.0, 4.5] miss band and therefore recorded as a miss rather than quietly absorbed. The audit's own pre-registered prediction for that number — ≈3.89 — was itself a miss, and is recorded as one.

Then a six-cell calibration gate at K=24 had to clear three legs before the other 18 cells were allowed to exist. It did: cross-entropy 10.903–11.223 → 3.772–4.585, finite, all six COMPLETED at 20,000 steps; in-distribution κ = 1.000 at h ∈ {1,2,3} in all six; deep κ at the top rung 0.9959 / 0.9959 / 0.9959 (frozen) and 0.9878 / 0.9674 / 0.9837 (trainable) against a bar of 0.90. A reconciliation rule the build audit had inserted — if the first calibration cell's realized cost diverges from the priced ratio by more than 15%, re-enter the pricing gate — fired at −18.4% and re-priced the wave downward before the sweep was released.

The runs were uneventful. 24 of 24 training cells COMPLETED at step 20,000, 0 failures, and 50 evaluation cells (24 breadth batteries, 24 depth-extension cells, 2 independent-draw re-measures), 0 failures. Measured cost, summed cell by cell from each record's own gpu_h field: 81.415 GPU-h of training — 13.791 at K=16, 17.207 at K=24, 22.817 at K=32, 27.601 at K=40 — plus 0.461 GPU-h across all 50 eval cells. Against the priced headline ledger of 110.91 GPU-h (99.78 priced for training alone), the realized training figure is 18.4% under — the reconciliation rule's own number, landing exactly. Per cell, the 392M cells cost 2.87×, 3.97× and 4.07× their 98M counterparts at K=16, 32 and 40 respectively, at 4.008× the parameters.

The attribution wave that followed was equally uneventful in training and equally instructive in bookkeeping. 12 of 12 cells COMPLETED at step 40,000, then 2 of 2 for V2′ — but four cells were first flagged FAILED by their own validity checker, which asserted config.steps_target == 40000 while the runner records steps_target at top level: the assert read None and called a flawless cell under-trained. The smoke test had missed it through assert-order masking — at three marginal steps the step ≥ 40000 clause fails first, so the steps_target clause was never exercised. The field path was corrected in all 24 spec instances, the corrected check re-executed against each cell's own artifact, and each cell moved failed → completed only on a genuine PASS (4 of 4). The training was never wrong; the checker was. That is the difference between "0 failures" and the phrase this page now uses, 0 unrecovered failures.

Two bookkeeping corrections belong here rather than in a footnote, under the house rule that produced the previous corrections on this program.

  • The wave's own cost. The run log recorded it at "≈69 GPU-h", a figure that extrapolated the K=24 calibration cell's cost to all 18 sweep cells. Per-cell cost scales with sequence length, and sequence length scales with K, so the true sum is 81.415 GPU-h. The number on this page is recomputed from the per-cell records; the log's projection is superseded.
  • Marginal versus cumulative, on the extensions. A resumed cell restores its parent's cumulative elapsed time from the checkpoint, so its recorded gpu_h covers the whole 40,000 steps, not the marginal 20,000. Summing the field across the 12 attribution cells gives 88.424 — which silently re-bills the 43.931 GPU-h already spent on their 20,000-step parents. The marginal spend is 44.493 GPU-h, and V2′'s is 9.287 (field 18.464 less 9.177 of parent). Anyone adding this chapter's ledger from the raw fields will over-count by exactly the parent generation.

So the chapter's recomputed, marginal ledger: 81.415 GPU-h for the 24-cell wave, 0.461 across its 50 eval cells, 44.493 for the 12-cell attribution arm and 9.287 for V2′ — ≈135.7 GPU-h all-in, 38 training cells at 392M and 76 eval passes, 0 unrecovered failures. That total crosses the chapter's own 130 GPU-h ledger gate, which V2′ tripped: the overrun was licensed by a recorded adjudication before the cells were queued — the grounds being that the verdict decides the chapter's central scale claim, that the marginal cost was ~7% of the remaining window, and that the alternative was publishing "stands, on a control we know was broken." It is reported as an overrun that was authorised, not as a budget that happened to fit.

Across the chapters that share this instrument, the program's own ledger records 106 training cells, ≈145 eval passes, 0 unrecovered failures and ≈180 GPU-h. The archives cited in §09 account for 83 of those cells and 176.467 GPU-h of that spend, recomputed cell by cell here on the marginal convention above; the balance sits in the earlier write-conditioning waves, whose training records are not in this chapter's archives and are not re-derived on this page.

08Limitations — read before citing

  • P1b is exact teacher-forced operator substitution, not a learned write. Every capability number here is measured with the operator computed in closed form from the true binding and fed into the trained checkpoint's own read path. It is a read-path capability result. The same checkpoints' own writes are §03's wall, and they are at chance.
  • The attribution control has run, and it removed one of the two degradations. The token budget is fixed across scales, so tokens-per-parameter falls from 1.87 to 0.47 at K=40, and under-training can only manufacture a degradation. At double the budget the K=32 trainable cells recover over the capability bar, so that verdict is withdrawn and reported as token-budget-limited; the K=40 trainable fall and the deep-tail degradation both survive it. Nothing in §06 should be cited as "the capability is scale-fragile" — the frozen arm's capability is at ceiling at every ported K at both scales, and what degrades is the trainable adapter at K=40 and the very deep read tail.
  • The extended arm is two seeds, not three. The 40,000-step cells are seeds 0 and 1 only, compared against 20,000-step medians over three seeds. A seed-matched sensitivity — restricting the 98M twin to the same two seeds — leaves 5 of the 6 depth-tail verdicts unchanged and flips only frozen K=32, the cell nearest the bar. The 2-versus-3-seed convention gap is recorded as a design gap of this chapter, not resolved by it.
  • The depth-tail row at K=40 trainable comes from the schedule-damaged cells. V2′ was scored on the breadth battery, not on the depth ladder, so the s* = 13 reading for K=40 trainable at 40,000 steps is a warm-restart cell. The verdict does not hinge on it: that cell's 20,000-step reading already missed the ±0.095 recovery band by 0.558.
  • "Double the tokens" is tokens seen, not loss moved — at K=40 in particular. Across the twelve extended cells the marginal segment moved training cross-entropy by a median of −0.125, but at K=40 trainable it moved almost nothing under either schedule (+0.026 / −0.055 under the warm restart, −0.048 / +0.005 at constant LR). Those cells are at a plateau, so the K=40 token-budget explanation is ruled out on budget delivered, not on further fitting achieved.
  • V2′ is a two-cell schedule control at one K, at one constant learning rate — the schedule's own 20,000-step floor, 3.00×10⁻⁵. It rules out this restart as the cause of this damage; it is not a learning-rate sweep, and the instrument finding in §06 is scoped accordingly.
  • Two model sizes is not a scaling law. 98M and 392M are two points. "Survives 4× scale" is exactly and only what was measured; it does not extrapolate to 1B, and no slope on this page is fitted.
  • Four breadths, not eight. K = 16, 24, 32, 40 of no. 19's eight are ported. K=12, 20, 28 and 36 have no 392M counterpart, and the K=44 probe is construction-impossible in the matched squaring band (3K/2 ≤ 63) — a design limit, not a capability limit, and nothing here speaks to K ≥ 44.
  • Three seeds per (K, recipe, scale). A per-K Mann–Whitney on 3 vs 3 has a minimum attainable two-sided p of 0.10, so no per-K significance claim is made or is possible; that is why every test here is stratified across K. Disclosed in the design, not discovered at harvest.
  • The K=24 stratum is the residual in the cross-scale test. Its 98M cells are an eval-only anchor trained under an older runner and re-scored onto this ladder. The design pre-registered the sensitivity that drops it and the rule that the 6-stratum verdict governs on disagreement — which is exactly the case that occurred in §02, and it is reported as the headline instrument note rather than buried.
  • The 8-stratum capability reading is tie-heavy and fragile. 14 of 72 pairs are exact ties (the ceiling effect), and it survives only 4 of 8 leave-one-stratum-out subsets. The governing verdict is the 6-stratum one; the 8-stratum number is reported because the design required both, not because it is the stronger instrument.
  • "No detectable shift" is not "no shift." It is the pre-registered band's label for a result inside the noise floor at this seed count and this stratum count. Per-K, 6 of 8 cells sit within ±0.05 and 2 do not.
  • The ordering result is a 4-stratum test. Finding no. 19 resolved the analogous fragility by extending to 8 strata; that resolution is unavailable here because only four K are ported. The design compensated by raising the confirmation threshold above the reference's own reading (T > 31.5) rather than by pretending the instrument is stronger than it is. 36/36 is the ceiling of the statistic, so it cannot be more than "perfect at this resolution."
  • A synthetic entity-binding task grafted into a real LM. The construction exists to exercise the NCR head. Nothing here claims the backbone uses this circuit in ordinary language modelling.
  • d = K+1 co-varies with K throughout. The variable is the pair, never K alone. §05's interaction is an interaction between parameter scale and the pair.
  • One eval seed for the curves of record (90210, n=256); the two re-measures use 31337. Seed variation is across training checkpoints, not across eval draws.
  • The deepest wall reading is within-392M only. The 24-of-24 in-band result at 15 squarings is labelled in every record as not comparable to the archived 98M P0 at 11 squarings.
