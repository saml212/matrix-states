## Leg 2 — external by-mechanism (Sonnet, web-verified): NOVEL
No published work combines (1) repeated-squaring matrix-power reads of
fast-weight state, (2) closed-form pseudoinverse writes inside a real

closest prior (cite as grounding): Schlag/Irie/Schmidhuber
arXiv:2102.11174 (fast weight programmers); Wang et al.

(shortcuts to automata — O(log T) depth via attention layers, not
fast-weight powers); Arora et al. arXiv:2312.04927 (Zoology/MQAR —
dimension-vs-K curves for FLAT recall only).
